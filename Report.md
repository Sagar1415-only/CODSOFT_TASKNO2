# Exploratory Data Analysis Report

## Dataset
Retail Sales Dataset

## Objective
To analyze the retail sales dataset using descriptive statistics and visualizations to identify trends, distributions, relationships, and business insights.

## Key Findings

- The dataset contains **1000 records** and **9 columns**.
- No missing values were found.
- No duplicate records were found after preprocessing.
- Customer ages are distributed across different age groups, with most customers belonging to the middle-age range.
- The **Total Amount** column contains a few high-value transactions, indicating outliers.
- Gender distribution is balanced between Male and Female customers.
- The three product categories (Beauty, Clothing, and Electronics) contribute differently to total sales.
- Correlation analysis shows the relationship between Quantity, Price per Unit, and Total Amount.
- Product-wise sales analysis helps identify the best-performing category.

## Conclusion

The dataset is clean and suitable for further analysis. Exploratory Data Analysis provided useful insights into customer demographics, purchasing behavior, product performance, and sales trends.
